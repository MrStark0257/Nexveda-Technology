1. Overview

Nexveda Technologies respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and while providing our services, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Through our contact form: full name, email address, phone number (optional), and project details
  • By email, phone or WhatsApp: any details you choose to share
  • For projects: business information, content, brand assets, and account access you provide so we can do the work
  • For billing: billing name, address, tax details (such as GSTIN), and payment references. We do not store your card or banking passwords.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and server/security logs

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries, usually within 24 hours
  • Prepare proposals and deliver, support and maintain our services
  • Send invoices and process payments
  • Communicate about your project and, where permitted, share updates about our services
  • Improve our website and services
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting obligations
  • Protect against fraud, abuse and security incidents

4. Legal basis and consent

We process your personal data based on your consent (for example when you submit our contact form), to perform a contract with you, to comply with the law, or for other legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, though this may affect our ability to provide services.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as hosting, email, project-management, design and payment tools, and only as needed for their service
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect legal rights
  • A successor, if our business is ever transferred, who would be bound by this policy

Some of these providers may be located outside India, so your information may be processed in other countries.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including to meet legal and accounting requirements. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are kept for up to 12 months. Project and billing records are kept for 8 years or as the law requires. After that we delete or anonymise them.

7. Data security

We use reasonable security measures, such as access controls, encrypted connections (SSL) and secure tools, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you may:

  • Ask for access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate data
  • Ask us to erase your data, where we no longer need it
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Raise a grievance and have it addressed
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to

To make a request, email nexvedatechnologies@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period required by law.

9. Cookies

Our website uses only essential browser storage (such as saving your light/dark theme preference) and does not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking cookies. You can control cookies and site storage through your browser settings.

10. Third-party links

Our website links to other sites, including demo projects and social profiles. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

11. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children under 18 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on our website with its “Last updated” date.

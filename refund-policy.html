1. General principle

Our services are custom work, and time and resources are committed as soon as a project starts. This policy explains what happens if you cancel or are unhappy.

2. Advance and milestone payments

  • The initial payment (advance) secures your place in our schedule and covers planning and discovery work. It is [non-refundable] once work has started.
  • Milestone payments are due when each stage is delivered and are not refundable once the stage has been delivered for your review.

3. Cancellation by the client

When you cancelWhat happens
Before any work has startedFull refund of the advance, less payment gateway or bank charges
During discovery or designYou pay for work completed so far, based on the proportion of the project finished. Any remaining advance is refunded.
After development has begunYou pay for work completed so far. The advance is not refunded.
After delivery or launchNo refund. Warranty fixes under the Terms still apply during the warranty period.

Cancellation requests must be sent in writing to nexvedatechnologies@gmail.com.

4. Cancellation or non-delivery by us

If we cancel a project or cannot deliver the agreed scope for reasons within our control, we will refund the amount you paid for work that has not been delivered.

5. Refunds for dissatisfaction

Because our work is custom and revisions are included, we do not give refunds simply because a design or approach is not to your taste after approval. If you are unhappy with a deliverable, tell us in writing within [7] days of delivery and we will work with you through the revision process in good faith.

6. Maintenance and subscription plans

  • Monthly maintenance plans are billed in advance.
  • You may cancel with [30] days’ written notice, and the plan ends at the end of the paid period.
  • Fees already paid for the current period are not refundable.

7. Third-party costs

Domains, hosting, licences, stock assets and other third-party purchases made on your behalf are non-refundable once purchased, as they are governed by the provider’s own terms.

8. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method or bank account within [7 to 10] business days of approval. Gateway or transfer fees may be deducted where they cannot be recovered.

9. Contact

For cancellation or refund requests, write to nexvedatechnologies@gmail.com or message +91-9824999054 on WhatsApp, with your name, project name and invoice number.
